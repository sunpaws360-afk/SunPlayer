SunPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026
Package: com.rebecca.sunplayer

The short version

SunPlayer is an offline-first music player. It has no Internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. It cannot send your data anywhere, even if you wanted it to.

Data SunPlayer processes

  • Audio files on your device. On Android 13+ SunPlayer requests READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (on older versions, storage read up to API 32) so it can list and play the music already stored on your device. It does not read documents, photos, messages, or call logs.
  • Library index, playlists, favourites, play counts and history. These are stored in a local database inside the app's private storage on your device. They never leave the device.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app declares no INTERNET permission, makes no network calls, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and performs no telemetry.

Cloud backup

If you have Android's built-in backup enabled, the OS may include the app's small local database (playlists/favourites) in your device backup according to Google's Android backup terms. You can disable this in system settings. Cached data and temporary files are excluded from backup.

Data deletion

  • Uninstalling SunPlayer removes its database and all app data immediately.
  • Your music files are never modified or deleted by the app.
  • Android “Clear app data” (system settings) wipes everything the app has ever created.

Children's privacy

SunPlayer collects no personal information from anyone, including children, because it processes everything locally and transmits nothing.

Changes to this policy

If future versions add optional network features (for example metadata lookup or AI), they will be opt-in with an explicit toggle, and this policy will be updated before such a release. The core player remains fully functional without any network access.
