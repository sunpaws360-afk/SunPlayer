# SunPlayer — Project Status & Roadmap

## Current Phase: Milestone 2 — Modern Player Experience & Enhanced Library (v1.2.0)
- **Baseline Version**: v1.1.0 (Local playback & MediaStore working on device)
- **Target in v1.2.0**:
  1. **Instant Search**: Real-time filtering by song title, artist, or album.
  2. **Multi-criteria Sorting**: Sort by Title, Artist, Album, or Duration.
  3. **Full Playback Controls**: Shuffle and 3-state Repeat (Off, Repeat All, Repeat One).
  4. **Expanded Now Playing View**: Tap mini-player to open modal sheet with large art, title/artist/album, seek slider with live timestamp scrub, and complete controls.
  5. **Solar Dark Theme**: Custom dark theme with warm gold/amber accents matching the "SunPlayer" identity.
